## Decisions
- A `variants` item is an option id, `decision=option`, or a scenario id. With no `variants`, the entry applies to every route. Out of route, a problem is `info`, never a warning.
- An identifier with neither `value` nor `values` is pending and valid. `localEnv.files` minItems is 0. Both changes are additive; schemaVersion stays 5.
- `support.planned` in Options is an extra count on top of the existing operations/files/unsupported counts, not a replacement for them.
- Q10 (PM): a cost line `none: "<reason>"` shows "no cloud cost (<reason>)". A missing declaration shows "unknown". Giving both `none` and a figure is an options.json warning.
